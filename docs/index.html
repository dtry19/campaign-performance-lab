Reporting quality & definitions

CTR = clicks / impressions. CVR = click-attributed conversions / clicks. CPA = spend / conversions. ROAS = attributed revenue / spend. Ratios use summed numerators and denominators; zero denominators display as —. Revenue is not profit. CTV/DOOH and view-through attribution are outside this project's scope.
